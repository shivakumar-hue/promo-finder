# Promo Finder v2

This version parses timecoded scene and camera/action/dialogue/sound blocks; scores hooks, endings, standalone context, energy, pacing, and length; proposes continuous and stitched clips; diversifies the shortlist; and verifies structural rules before ranking. Speaker aliases use generic honorific removal and conservative name-token subset matching, with safeguards against merging family roles or differently titled people by surname alone.

## Run it

```powershell
python promo_finder.py "PATH_TO_UNZIPPED_EPISODES" --out out --top 30 --per-ep 3
```

Input may contain `.txt` files in subfolders. The parser reads `EPISODE NUMBER`, `EPISODE LOGLINE`, and optional `CONTINUITY NOTES`, plus timecoded beats formatted `Start: (MM:SS.xx) | End: (MM:SS.xx)`. Scene start/end labels are case-insensitive.

## Outputs

- `promos.json` — selected promos, segments, component scores, source quotes, structural verification, and quality flags. It is the source of truth.
- `promos.csv` — generated by reading back `promos.json`.
- `best_per_episode.csv` and `.json` — the best structurally valid candidate for every episode, including episodes outside the top 30.
- `episode_coverage.csv` and `.json` — runtime, scene count, selected-promo coverage, best-per-episode score, and episode summaries.
- `data_qa.json` — source timing warnings, including camera gaps/overlaps and dialogue overlaps.

## Current run

On this 50-episode screenplay set, the tool considered **10,220 sampled candidates**, filtered out 106 structural failures, and selected 30 promos: 18 continuous, 8 same-episode stitched, and 4 cross-episode. The shortlist covers 26 episodes; the fallback list covers every episode. Estimated script runtime is **01:42:42.62**.

The bundled regression suite passed **23 checks**, including eight known ending cases, speaker-alias safeguards, JSON/CSV consistency, and verbatim hook/cliffhanger checks against the correct episode and segment.

## What “verified” means

A selected promo passes checks for a source block at the opening, a source block near the ending, source episode membership, 20–60 seconds of combined media, and no dialogue sliced by a cut. The regression suite separately checks exact source text and episode/time-range membership. Neither check confirms acting, finished coverage, pacing, standalone clarity, or viewer response. Check `quality_flags` and watch every proposed segment.

## Editorial limits

- Scores are heuristics, not probabilities. Selection applies diminishing returns to spread clips across episodes, so displayed scores may not strictly descend.
- Candidate generation keeps a strong opening per four-second time bucket and samples representative durations/endings; it does not test every possible cut.
- Camera-note gaps are warnings with a capped score penalty, not proof that footage is missing. Verify against the finished show.
- Stitches require a shared speaker between segments, which filters some unrelated pairs but cannot prove story coherence. Check the seam.
- The per-episode fallback improves visibility of finale moments but does not boost them into the overall top 30.
- The scoring has not been evaluated on another show or genre; no labeled precision/recall set exists yet.
- No LLM/API reranking was used in this run.
